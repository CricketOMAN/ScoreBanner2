Use Parameter What it does
matchId The CricClubs match to follow (required for live matches).
clubId CricClubs club ID. Defaults to 40319 (USACricketJunior).
theme One of the themes above. Defaults to orange.
logo Shows a sponsor logo in the top-right corner (1 or 2).
debug Sample data instead of a live match: 1 first innings, 2 chase, 3 result, 4 toss, 5 no logos.
card In debug mode, holds a sample card on screen (wicket, milestone, partnership, boundary).
mode=replay Cycles through a recorded match to test transitions.
quiet Turns off the event cards (wicket, fifty, partnership, boundary) and shows only the bar.
